Design DRI and group lead

Notetaker + Smart Deal Progression

Problem
Reps lost hours after every call to CRM admin, and every HubSpot AI bet needed the same missing input: what customers actually said.
How I drove it
Led the design of HubSpot's system for turning sales conversations into next steps, CRM updates, and follow-up. I set the quality bar, the research plan, and the operating rhythm across three groups on a fixed launch date, all while helping teams adjust to a new sprint model of working.
Outcome
AI reps trust, because every suggestion shows its source. Deals closed faster, post-call admin shrank, and adoption hit record highs; customers report saving 1.5 hours per rep, per day. We met every post-launch target in the first month.

Founding team member, co-led design and research

Veeva Engage

Problem
When COVID closed clinics, field reps lost their way to reach doctors, in one of the most regulated industries there is.
How I drove it
Was part of a team the CEO assembled to build a startup inside Veeva when COVID closed clinics, co-leading design. Director of UX

Veeva Network

Problem
100+ data stewards worked all day in a dense, multi-tab database grid built without designers, and there was no UX practice to change it.
How I drove it
Built the design practice for a life-sciences data platform from the ground up, in an org that had only worked with a junior designer: a UX process every feature followed, hiring, research, a design library, and the company's first UX internship program.
Outcome
A profile design that became the platform's foundation: empty fields hidden for faster scanning, inline record resolution, and a live preview for admins. Network now serves 45K+ field users, and Veeva reports change requests validated in hours, not the 10+ day industry average. Designers I grew went on to Autodesk, Amazon, and Bloomberg.
